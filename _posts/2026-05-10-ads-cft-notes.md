The AdS/CFT correspondence, also known as the Maldacena duality, is a conjectured relationship between two kinds of physical theories.

## Key Idea

> AdS/CFT is a duality between a theory of quantum gravity in (d+1)-dimensional Anti-de Sitter space and a conformal field theory on its d-dimensional boundary.

## Historical Context

The correspondence was first proposed by Juan Maldacena in 1997 and has since become one of the most important developments in theoretical physics.

## Basic Setup

- **Bulk**: Type IIB string theory on $AdS_5 \times S^5$
- **Boundary**: $\mathcal{N}=4$ supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory in 4D
